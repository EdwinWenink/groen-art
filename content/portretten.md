## About Section
The site explains that "Groen-art" refers to the artist's surname rather than ecological themes. Marjo studied drawing, painting, and etching at Vrije Academie in Zevenaar and atelier "De Waan," working with various materials including pencil, charcoal, watercolor, oil, and acrylic. She notes that "mijn kleurige schilderijen uit de beginperiode vrolijkheid" [colorful early paintings brought joy] while she now focuses on nature's mysterious aspects.

## Featured Works
