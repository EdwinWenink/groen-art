## About the Artist
The site describes an artist portfolio. The artist explains that "Groen-art" refers to her surname, though nature significantly influences her work. She trained at Vrije Academie in Zevenaar and atelier "De Waan," developing skills in drawing, painting, and etching across multiple mediums and styles ranging from realistic to abstract.

## Featured Artworks
1. **Atjeh-strijder** — A commissioned book cover for Bert Wenink's novel about the Aceh War, described as "de langste en bloedigste oorlog uit Nederlands" (the longest and bloodiest war in Dutch colonial history).

2. **Boetius en de skyline van Rotterdam** — A commissioned acrylic work (80x240) depicting Rotterdam's skyline, created for former Feyenoord footballer Jean Paul Boëtius.
