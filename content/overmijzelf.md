# Over Mijzelf (About Me)

## Main Heading
"De Schilderijen van Marjo Groen!" (The Paintings of Marjo Groen)

## About Section
The artist explains that "Groen-art" derives from her surname, Marjo Groen. She emphasizes that nature serves as inspiration for her work, particularly in depicting animals and abstract forms derived from natural phenomena.

Her training includes studies at Vrije Academie in Zevenaar and atelier "De Waan," where she learned various techniques using materials like charcoal, watercolor, oil, and acrylic across realist and abstract styles.
