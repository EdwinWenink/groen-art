## Primary Content
The site introduces Marjo Groen, an artist whose name inspired the website's title. While the name "Groen" means green, the artist explains that "natuur wel degelijk een inspiratiebron voor mijn schilderijen" (nature is indeed an inspiration source for paintings). She paints animals and abstract forms derived from natural phenomena.

Training included drawing, painting, and etching at the Free Academy in Zevenaar and atelier "De Waan," working with various media and techniques ranging from realistic to abstract.

The artist notes her earlier colorful work brought joy, while she now feels drawn to exploring nature's mysterious aspects through increased abstraction.
