## About Section
The site explains that "Groen-art" refers to the artist's surname rather than ecological themes. Marjo studied drawing, painting, and etching at Vrije Academie in Zevenaar and atelier "De Waan," working with diverse materials including charcoal, watercolor, oil, and acrylics across realistic to abstract styles.

Her artistic journey reveals an evolution: early colorful works conveyed cheerfulness, while recent pieces explore "the mysterious side of nature," encouraging fresh perspectives through abstraction. The artist accepts commissioned works.
