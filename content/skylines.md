## About Section
The artist explains that "Groen-art" refers to her surname rather than ecological themes, though nature serves as creative inspiration. She studied drawing, painting, and etching at Vrije Academie in Zevenaar and atelier "De Waan," working with diverse materials including charcoal, watercolor, oil, and acrylics across realistic to abstract styles.

Her artistic journey reveals an evolution: early colorful works conveyed cheerfulness, while recent pieces explore "the mysterious side of nature," encouraging fresh perspectives through abstraction. The artist accepts commissioned works.

## Artwork Descriptions
**Atjeh-strijder**: Book cover for "Overgeven of sneuvelen" depicting a Dutch colonial-era conflict described as "the longest and bloodiest war" in Netherlands history.

**Boëtius and Rotterdam Skyline**: Commissioned acrylic on canvas (80x240cm) featuring an ex-Feyenoord footballer with the city's skyline.
