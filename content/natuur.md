## Main Content
The page introduces Marjo Groen's art website. The artist explains that "Groen-art" refers to her surname rather than ecological themes, though nature serves as inspiration for her work. She paints animals and abstract forms derived from natural phenomena.

Her training includes drawing, painting, and etching at the Vrije Academie in Zevenaar and atelier "De Waan," working with various materials including pencil, charcoal, chalk, ink, watercolor, oil, and acrylic. She practices techniques ranging from realism to abstraction.

The artist notes her colorful early paintings brought joy, while she's currently drawn to exploring nature's mysterious aspects through greater abstraction. She accepts commissions and invites contact via email or phone.
