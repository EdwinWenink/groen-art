## Primary Content

The website introduces Marjo Groen, an artist whose site name derives from her surname rather than ecological themes, though nature serves as significant inspiration. She paints animals and abstract forms influenced by natural phenomena.

Groen studied drawing, painting, and etching at Vrije Academie in Zevenaar and atelier "De Waan," working with diverse materials including pencil, charcoal, chalk, ink, watercolor, oil, and acrylic. She practices techniques ranging from realism to abstraction.

Her artistic evolution moved from "colorful paintings from the beginning period" bringing joy to exploring nature's mysterious aspects through increased abstraction. The artist accepts commissions via email or phone.
