## About Section
The site explains that "Groen-art" refers to the artist's surname rather than ecological themes. Marjo studied drawing, painting, and etching at Vrije Academie in Zevenaar and atelier "De Waan," working with various materials and techniques ranging from realistic to abstract styles.

Her artistic journey has evolved: "mijn kleurige schilderijen uit de beginperiode vrolijkheid" (colorful early works brought joy), while she is currently drawn to nature's mysterious aspects and abstract interpretation.

The artist accepts commissions and welcomes personal contact via email or mobile.
