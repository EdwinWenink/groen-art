## About Section
The artist explains that "Groen-art" refers to her surname rather than ecological themes, though nature serves as creative inspiration. She studied drawing, painting, and etching at Vrije Academie in Zevenaar and atelier "De Waan," working with various media including pencil, charcoal, watercolor, oil, and acrylic. Her artistic evolution moved from colorful, joyful works to increasingly abstract explorations of nature's mysterious aspects. She accepts commission work and invites inquiries via contact methods.

## Featured Artwork
- **Atjeh-strijder**: A book cover commissioned for novelist Bert Wenink depicting the Aceh War, described as the longest and bloodiest conflict in Dutch colonial history.
- **Boetius en de skyline van Rotterdam**: An acrylic painting (80x240) created for former Feyenoord footballer Jean Paul Boëtius featuring Rotterdam's skyline.
