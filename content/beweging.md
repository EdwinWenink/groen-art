## About Section
The site explains that "Groen-art" refers to the artist's surname rather than ecological themes, though nature serves as genuine inspiration. The artist studied drawing, painting, and etching at two Dutch academies, working with diverse materials including pencil, charcoal, watercolor, oil, and acrylic. Her practice spans realistic to abstract techniques.

Her artistic evolution moved from "kleurige schilderijen uit de beginperiode" (vibrant early paintings) toward more mysterious natural subjects with increased abstraction. She accepts commission work.

## Featured Works
**Atjeh-strijder** – A commissioned book cover for a novel about the Aceh War, described as "de langste en bloedigste oorlog uit Nederlands koloniale geschiedenis."

**Boetius en de skyline van Rotterdam** – An 80x240cm acrylic commission featuring a former Feyenoord footballer and Rotterdam's skyline.
