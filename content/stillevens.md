## About Section
The site presents an artist portfolio. The artist explains that "Groen-art" refers to her surname rather than ecological themes. Nature serves as inspiration, with focus on animals and abstract forms derived from natural phenomena.

Training included studies at the Vrije Academie in Zevenaar and atelier "De Waan," covering drawing, painting, and etching with various materials and techniques from realism to abstraction.

## Notable Works
1. **Atjeh-strijder** – Book cover for Bert Wenink's historical novel about the Aceh War
2. **Boetius and Rotterdam Skyline** – Commission featuring former Feyenoord player Jean Paul Boëtius, executed in acrylic (80x240cm)
